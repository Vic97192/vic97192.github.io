About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Cheng Kung University, Taiwan, with a minor in Mathematics.

My research interests lie at the intersection of trustworthy machine learning, black-box model extraction, and AI systems. I investigate how query distributions affect knowledge transfer, and whether useful query strategies can be learned from limited feedback under distribution shift.

Beyond research, I enjoy building practical systems and contributing to technical communities. I currently serve as Chief Technology Officer of GDG on Campus NCKU and previously founded an AI research group at Hsinchu Senior High School.

What I Do

  • Research: I study black-box machine-learning systems, out-of-distribution queries, teacher–student knowledge transfer, and representation structure.
  • Engineering: I enjoy translating ideas into working systems, including an augmented-reality exhibition prototype tested in a physical environment.
  • Community and Teaching: I contribute to student developer communities and programming education.
